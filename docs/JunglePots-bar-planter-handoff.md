# Bar Planter — collection page

The thirteenth and last. Every collection in the catalogue now has a page.

---

## 1. The only collection with no specification at all

The supplied catalogue gives Bar Planter **one photograph and nothing else** —
no dimensions, no capacity, no material description, no finish code, no
variants. Not incomplete ones: none. Jungle Flora has no listing for it either.

So this page is built the other way round from the rest, and three parts of the
template are **removed rather than left empty**:

**No schedule.** Six rows reading "not published" would look like a
specification with gaps. The section is titled *What is published*, the heading
is "Nothing, and that is the point", and the absence is stated once in prose.

**No drawing.** Every other page traces the catalogue's own photograph and
checks the trace against a published dimension. Here there is no dimension to
check against, and the object is half-hidden behind its own planting and a
blurred foreground leaf. A trace would be an invention. The drawing plate
carries a sentence explaining that instead — which is a truer thing to put in
that space than a guess.

**No colour picker.** The four swatches are the planter range's, and this
collection is not described by them (see §3). Leaving a picker on a page that
says "ask for the actual finish" would contradict the page, so the swatches are
gone and the enquiry form reads "Colour reference: to be agreed".

---

## 2. Observation, kept separate from specification

What the photograph shows is written down in its own disclosure, labelled as
observation and explicitly not a basis for ordering:

| | |
|---|---|
| Form | A circular worktop at about bar height, on a flared pedestal |
| Planting | An aperture cut through the centre of the top, planted through |
| Finish | A red-brown, which is none of the four named swatches |
| Above it | A pole through the planting carrying a pendant lamp |
| Setting | An office tea point, with chairs and a banquette around it |

The point of writing it down is to give the client something concrete to be
asked about, rather than an empty page. Two items are worth putting directly to
Jungle Flora:

* **Is the pole and pendant lamp part of the product?** It rises through the
  planting and is lit in the photograph. It may equally be the building's.
* **What is the red-brown?** See below.

---

## 3. A finding for the whole finish range

Bar Planter is photographed in a **red-brown that is none of the four named
colours**. The catalogue analysis already recorded that *"the red-brown finish
in Big Ben Tower photography is not assigned a colour code"*.

So the same unnamed red-brown appears on at least two collections, and the
published four-swatch range — Anthracite 7016, Pale Green 6021, Cappuccino
1014, Pure white 9010 — **does not describe what the catalogue itself shows**.
Counting what has turned up across these thirteen pages, the photography
contains at least: dark grey, a pale sage green, a pale green, a beige, and
this red-brown. Two of those five are unnamed.

That belongs in the same content request as the capacities: the finish range as
published is incomplete, not just unlabelled.
